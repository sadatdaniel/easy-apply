Three looks for Easy Apply

Same app, same layout, three sets of colours and fonts. Only the app around your documents changes; resume themes (Classic, Sidebar, Serif, Academic) stay as they are. Switch with the tabs, or scroll to compare.

A · Graphite

Neutral greys with one cobalt-blue accent, Inter throughout. Calm and familiar, like a well-made desktop tool; the documents carry the colour.
